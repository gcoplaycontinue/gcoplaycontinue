Getting Started

Complete a Payment at g.co/playcontinue

g.co/playcontinue is a Google Play shortcut link. It helps you finish a purchase or subscription you started on your TV. Sign in with the same Google email address shown on the TV’s payment screen so you can find the purchase you started.

If your TV shows g.co/playcontinue, leave that screen open. Pick up your phone or use a computer, open a web browser, and type g.co/playcontinue into the address bar. Once you finish the steps on Google’s page, go back to your TV.

Step 1. Choose a plan or free trial on your TV

Open the app on your Android TV, Google TV, or other compatible device. Go to the subscription or premium section and choose the plan you want.

For example, the LooLoo Kids – Nursery Rhymes screen showed two offers:

  • 7 days free, then $24.99 per year
  • 3 days free, then $2.99 per month

These are examples from one app screen. Check the trial length, price, and renewal date shown for your account before selecting a plan.

Step 2. Scan the QR code or open the address

  • Scan the QR code. If your TV shows a QR code, point your phone’s camera at it and tap the link that appears on your phone.
  • Type the address. Open a browser on your phone, tablet, or computer and enter g.co/playcontinue in the address bar.

Sign in with the same Google email address shown on the TV’s payment screen. If your browser is already signed in with a different email, switch accounts. If your TV offers a Google Play Store notification on an Android device, you can also open that notification to continue.

Step 3. Review the subscription details

Look for the subscription you selected on the TV. In the LooLoo Kids example, the page showed FREE Trial + FULL Access with options to view details or subscribe. Select See details if it appears, then check the app name, trial length, billing amount, and how often the plan renews. If everything matches, select Subscribe to move to the payment screen.

Step 4. Choose or add a payment method

Select an available payment method on Google Play. If you see Add card, enter an eligible debit or credit card on Google’s page and complete any check your bank requests. If you see Redeem code, you can use an eligible Google Play code if the purchase supports it. The options you see depend on your country, account, and purchase.

A free trial can still ask for a payment method. Check the amount due today and the date your paid plan begins before you continue.

Step 5. Confirm the payment and return to your TV

Check the final total, then choose Subscribe, Buy, or the confirmation button Google Play shows. Complete any last payment check and wait for Google Play to confirm the purchase or trial.

Return to the app on your TV and check whether the paid features are available. If the app still shows the old screen, close and reopen it. Before paying again, check your receipt or Google Play order history.

If you started a free trial, check when it ends. To avoid the subscription charge, cancel before the trial expires. See Google Play’s subscription and free trial help.

Frequently asked questions

Can I continue without scanning the QR code?

Yes. Open a browser on your phone, tablet, or computer and type g.co/playcontinue into the address bar. Keep your TV on the payment screen while you do this.

Why can’t I see the purchase I started?

Compare the Google email address in the browser with the one shown on your TV’s payment screen. If they differ, switch to the account shown on your TV and check again.

Why does a free trial ask for a payment method?

Google Play may ask for a payment method for future charges. Before you subscribe, check what you pay today, when the trial ends, and what the plan will cost after that.

What if I paid and the TV app still asks me to subscribe?

Close and reopen the TV app. If it still asks you to pay, check your Google Play receipt or order history before trying again.

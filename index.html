If your TV displays g.co/playcontinue, use your phone or computer to follow the Google prompt, then return to your TV.

Use your Google account and any TV code only on the official Google page.

Continue from your TV

  1. Keep the TV prompt open

    Leave your TV on the screen that shows the link so you can refer to its instructions.

  2. Open the link on another device

    In your phone or computer browser, open g.co/playcontinue if that is the address displayed on the TV.

  3. Follow the Google prompt

    Choose the account you want to use if Google asks. If the Google page asks for a code, enter the code shown on your own TV.

  4. Return to the TV

    After you finish on the Google page, check the TV for the result or the next instruction.

Troubleshooting

The code has expired or was rejected

Return to the TV app and start the action again. If the TV displays a fresh code, use that one on the Google page.

The Google page opens the wrong account

Check the profile shown on the Google page and switch to the account you intended to use. For a purchase, check the selected account before confirming.

The TV shows a different link

Follow the instructions displayed in the TV app. For a third-party app, look up the link in that app’s official support documentation.

I want to redeem a Google Play gift card

Use the separate Google Play gift card redemption instructions.
